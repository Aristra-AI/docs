A chat on the website, your phone or Aristra Desktop's Chat tab runs in Aristra Cloud and is kept in your account, so it is the same chat everywhere you sign in. Chats in a folder on your computer are [Aristra Code](/surfaces/aristra-code) chats.

## Start a chat

- **On the web or your phone:** **New chat** in the sidebar of [app.aristralabs.com](https://app.aristralabs.com).
- **In Aristra Desktop:** a new chat in the **Chat** view. You need to be signed in.
- **From anywhere on your Mac:** press **⌥Space** for quick entry, type, and press Enter.
- **In a [project](/projects):** start it from the project, and it uses the project's instructions, files and memory.
- **From Telegram:** with a plan. See [Telegram](/surfaces/telegram).

## Choose the model

The model picker in the message box, beside the send button, lists Aristra's models: Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol, and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro. **Auto** uses Gemini 3.8 Flash. Each model shows how costly it is ($ to $$$), and some let you choose how hard it thinks (Low, Medium, High, and on some Extra High and Max). Every model is open on every account.

Each message is paid from your balance: a new account's starter credit first, then a plan's monthly usage, then top-ups. Prices are on [Models and prices](https://app.aristralabs.com/models). When your balance runs out, the message box says so and offers a top-up or a plan.

## What Aristra can do in a chat

| | |
|---|---|
| **Search and read the web** | Search the web, news, images and videos, and read pages. The **+** menu has switches for **Web search** and **URL extraction** |
| **Read your files** | Attachments, the files in your account, and PDFs page by page |
| **Run code** | In its own Linux sandbox: analyse data, and make PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files and charts |
| **Make images** | Choose **Create image** in the **+** menu, with a style |
| **Show things** | Charts and diagrams, weather, maps, comparison tables, and email drafts (it can't send them) |
| **Remember** | Save, update and forget memories as you talk. See [Memory](/memory) |
| **Ask you** | A question, or a short form, when it needs your answer |
| **Work in your apps** | With a plan, the apps you connected. See [Connectors](/connectors) |
| **Write docs** | With a plan, [Craft](/craft) docs beside the chat |
| **Schedule work** | With a plan, [scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks) |

## Attach files

Choose **+**, then **Upload files or images**, or **Attach from Files** for one already in your account. A message can carry up to 5 files, 50 MB each and 50 MB in all. Aristra reads PDF (scanned pages too), Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text, CSV, JSON, code and images.

A file you attach is kept with its chat, and counts toward your [storage](/files#storage), until you delete the chat.

## Edit and regenerate

Hover over a message to edit it, or over a reply to regenerate it. Each version is kept: arrows on the message move between them.

## Incognito chats

Choose the ghost icon at the top of the chat on the web, or on a new chat in Aristra Desktop's Chat tab. In the terminal, run `aristra --cloud --incognito`. An incognito chat:

- Is not in your chat list, and nothing from it is saved to your memory or learned from later.
- Can still use what Aristra already remembers about you, while Memory is on.
- Can't be opened again once you leave it.
- Is deleted 30 days after its last message.

## Find a chat

- **On the web:** the **Chats** page lists every chat and finds them by title. **⌘K** opens your recent chats, projects and actions. **⌘F** finds words in the open chat.
- **In Aristra Desktop:** **⌘K** finds chats by title, folders and commands, and words in the messages of this Mac's chats.

## Export or delete

A chat's menu has **Rename**, **Export as Markdown**, **Copy transcript (Markdown)** and **Delete**. Deleting a chat removes it and its files. To take a copy of every chat at once, see [Export](/account/export).

A chat can't be shared as a public link. To share something Aristra wrote, publish a [Craft](/craft) doc, or a file Aristra made: **Publish** on its card gives it a public link.

## Voice

In Aristra Desktop, the microphone button in the message box takes up to 10 minutes of dictation and types it for you, paid from your balance at $0.001 a minute. On [Telegram](/surfaces/telegram), Aristra reads voice notes.
