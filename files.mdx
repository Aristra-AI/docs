Your account keeps the files you add, the files you attach to chats, and the files Aristra makes for you. They show in **Files** on the website, in Aristra Desktop, and with `/files` in the terminal. Every account has room for them.

## On the website

**Files** in the sidebar lists every file your account keeps, newest first, with the chat each came from. There are no folders: filters do that job.

- **Whose files:** yours, or one [project's](/projects).
- **Filters:** **All files**, **Added by you**, **Attached to chats** and **Made by Aristra**; by kind (images, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, web pages); and **Starred**, **Recent** and **Recently deleted**.
- **Search** looks inside your files' text first, files attached to chats included, then at their names. Nothing is indexed with AI: a file is read when you search.
- **Add files** with the upload button, or by dropping them on the page.
- **Rename, star, delete and restore** a file you added or Aristra made. A file attached to a chat is kept with its chat: it is listed and found by name, but renamed or deleted only with its chat.
- **Published pages** lists the [Craft](/craft) docs and the files you published at a public link. Publishing comes with a plan; unpublishing never needs one.

A deleted file goes to **Recently deleted** and is removed for good after 7 days.

## In Aristra Desktop

**Files** in the sidebar lists what your chats on this Mac shared with you, made or changed, and the files you attached, newest first, with the chat each came from. Nothing is copied: a file opens where it is, beside the list, in its app, or in Finder, and **Go to the chat** opens its chat. A file that was moved or deleted shows as gone.

Search by name, folder or chat title, or press Return to search inside the files: their words, from an index kept on this Mac (no AI, nothing sent), with the page of a PDF.

Signed in, Files shows the files in your account too: **All**, **On this Mac** or **In your account**. A file in your account downloads when you open it. Upload with the button or by dropping files on the list, rename, star, and send files to the trash and back. Return in the search box searches inside your account's files too.

## Save a file from your Mac to your account

A file on your Mac reaches your account only when you save it: **Save to your account** in its menu, or ask Aristra to save it. The file stays on your Mac, shows once, marked **In your account**, and when it changes it offers **Update in your account**. If its copy was also changed on the web since, Aristra asks whether to replace that copy or keep both. Deleting either one never deletes the other. Without a connection, a save waits and goes when Aristra is reachable.

## In the terminal

`/files` lists the same files as Aristra Desktop. `/files find <words>` searches inside them, `/files open <n>` opens one, and `/files save <path>` and `/files update <n>` save a file to your account or update its copy.

## Storage

Your files and the files attached to your chats count toward your account's storage:

| Free | Plus | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 GB | 10 GB | 100 GB | 200 GB |

A file can be up to 50 MB. When your storage is full, a new file is refused and says so, whether you add it, attach it to a chat or restore it from **Recently deleted**; a file Aristra makes then isn't kept, and Aristra tells you. Nothing you have is removed. Remove files, or choose a [plan](/account/plans) with more room. With a plan, **Storage** in your account shows how much is used; without one, no page shows it yet.
