Aristra is one assistant with one account. Talk to it on the web or your phone, in Aristra Desktop on your Mac, in your terminal, or from Telegram. Your chats in Aristra Cloud, what Aristra remembers about you and your files follow you from one to the next.

## What Aristra does

  <Card title="Answers, research and writing" icon="magnifying-glass" href="/chats">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Work done for you" icon="chart-column" href="/chats#what-aristra-can-do-in-a-chat">
    It runs code in its own sandbox to analyse data and make files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, charts), and makes images.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Memory" icon="brain" href="/memory">
    It remembers what you tell it, learns from your recent chats each night, and lets you read and change all of it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your apps" icon="plug" href="/connectors">
    With a plan, it works in Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, Notion, GitHub and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work on a schedule" icon="clock" href="/scheduled-tasks">

## Where you use it

Each place you use Aristra is a surface. They share your account, so a chat in Aristra Cloud is there in each of them. A chat Aristra Code runs on your computer stays on that computer unless you share it with Remote Control.

| Surface | What it is |
|---|---|
| [The web and your phone](/surfaces/web) | [app.aristralabs.com](https://app.aristralabs.com), in any browser and on your phone. Your account's pages are here too |
| [Aristra Desktop](/surfaces/desktop) | The app for Mac: a **Chat** tab for your conversations and a **Code** tab for Aristra Code |
| [Aristra Code](/surfaces/aristra-code) | Aristra working in folders on your computer: the `aristra` command in your terminal, Aristra Desktop's Code tab and your editor |
| [Telegram](/surfaces/telegram) | Message Aristra from Telegram, with a plan |
| [Cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) | A Linux computer of your own in Aristra's cloud, which Aristra works on while your own computer is off. It needs a plan or money you added; the starter credit doesn't pay for it |

## What it costs

Every account is free to make and starts with a $1 credit for Aristra's models and tools. Chats on the web are paid from your balance as you go, and your memory and 1 GB of files come with every account. Aristra Desktop and the Aristra CLI are free with a model of your own: your own provider key, your ChatGPT plan, or a model on your computer.

A plan (Plus $20, Pro $100 or Max $200 a month) adds that much usage of Aristra's models each month, plus Craft, connectors, scheduled tasks in the cloud, Telegram, and using your computers from the web. See [Your account and Aristra One](/account/overview) and [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).
