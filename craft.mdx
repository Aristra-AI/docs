Craft is where you write docs, with Aristra or by yourself: a brief, a report, a plan, notes. Aristra writes in a doc beside your chat, and every change it makes is kept as a version you can undo.

Craft comes with every account, with or without a [plan](/account/plans): your docs are kept in your account and open on the web, your phone and Aristra Desktop. When Aristra writes in a doc from a chat, the chat is paid as any chat is, from your [balance](/account/credits) or your plan's usage.

## Start a doc

- **On the website:** **Craft** in the sidebar, or inside a [project](/projects). Start one with **New doc**, choose **Draft with Aristra** and describe it so Aristra writes it beside the chat, or **Import a file** (Markdown or plain text).
- **In Aristra Desktop:** **Craft** in the Chat tab's sidebar, or **New Craft doc** in a chat's `/` menu, which opens it beside the chat. You need to be signed in.
- **In a chat:** ask Aristra to write one.

Aristra asks before it edits a doc that wasn't made in the chat, opened beside it, or approved for it.

## Comments

On the website and in Aristra Desktop, select text and comment on it (a doc kept on your Mac has no comments). New comments wait so you can leave several, then **Send 2 comments to Aristra** (the button counts them) sends them together, and Aristra answers and edits. Reply to a comment, resolve it, and show resolved ones again.

## Versions

Your edits are kept as a version when you pause, and a version is kept before each change Aristra makes: **Version N**, when, and by whom. Preview a version, restore it as a new version, copy it as a new doc, or **Show changes since** it. **Undo Aristra's change** takes back Aristra's last edit.

## Export

| On the website | In Aristra Desktop |
|---|---|
| PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML and plain text, **Copy as Markdown**, and **Save to Files** | PDF, Markdown and a web page |

## Publish

**Share** publishes a read-only snapshot of a doc at a private link that anyone with it can read. Edits after you publish don't show until you publish again, and publishing again changes the link. **Unpublish** stops the link working for everyone. Published docs are listed under **Published pages** in [Files](/files).

## Docs on your Mac and in your account

A doc you start in Aristra Desktop while it can't reach your account is kept on your Mac, marked **On this Mac**, and so are the docs it kept there before Craft came with every account. Such a doc opens, edits, keeps its versions and exports with no connection, and Aristra can write in it from a chat on your Mac. It has no comments or published link.

Your account's docs show in Aristra Desktop too, and docs you opened before open offline. **Move to your account** in a doc's menu (or **Move all** at the top of Craft) puts a doc kept on your Mac into your account, with everything written in it, so it opens on the web and your phone. Nothing moves unless you ask.

When a plan ends, your docs stay in your account, and you keep writing in them.
