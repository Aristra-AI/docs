## Your account

Your Aristra account holds what follows you everywhere: your chats in the cloud, your [memory](/memory), your [files](/files), your [projects](/projects), your [Craft](/craft) docs, your [connected apps](/connectors), and your balance and plan. Every surface you sign in to reaches the same account.

## Where a chat runs

A chat runs in one of three places:

| | Aristra Cloud | Your computer | Your cloud computer |
|---|---|---|---|
| What runs it | Aristra's cloud agent | The Aristra Runtime, on your Mac or Linux machine | The Aristra Runtime, on your own computer in Aristra's cloud |
| Started from | The website, your phone, Aristra Desktop's Chat tab, Telegram, scheduled tasks | The terminal, Aristra Desktop's Code tab, your editor | Its page on the website, Telegram, its own scheduled tasks |
| What it works with | Its own sandbox, the web, your files and connected apps | Your folders, your shell and your tools | Its own disk, shell and tools |
| Models | Aristra's models | Any: Aristra's, your own keys, your ChatGPT plan, a model on your computer | Aristra's models, or your ChatGPT plan |
| Who pays | Your balance | Your balance for Aristra's models; nothing with your own model | Your balance, for its models and for its time awake |
| Kept | In your account | On your computer (`~/.aristra`) | In your account, or on its own disk if you choose |

Chats in Aristra Cloud are the same chats on every surface: one started on the web shows in Aristra Desktop's Chat tab and in the terminal's [cloud chats](/code/cloud-chats).

A chat on your computer stays there. With a plan, [Remote Control](/code/remote-control) shares it to the web and your phone while the work keeps running on your computer.

## Aristra Code

[Aristra Code](/surfaces/aristra-code) is Aristra working in a folder on your computer: it reads and edits files, runs commands and works with git, asking before anything you haven't allowed. The terminal, Aristra Desktop's Code tab and your editor all talk to the same runtime on your computer, so a chat started in one shows in the others. [How Aristra Code works](/code/how-it-works) has the details.

## What follows you, and what stays

- **Memory** is kept in your account and reaches every chat you start signed in, on every surface, unless you turn it off. See [Memory](/memory).
- **Your profile** (your name, occupation and **About you**) starts every chat, except an incognito chat in Aristra Code and a cloud computer that keeps its memory on its own disk.
- **Files** in your account show on the website, in Aristra Desktop and with `/files` in the terminal. A file on your Mac reaches your account only when you save it there. See [Files](/files).
- **Chats on your computer** are not in your account unless you share them with Remote Control, so Aristra's nightly learning from your chats doesn't read them.

## Who pays

Aristra's models and hosted tools are paid from your balance: a new account's $1 starter credit first, then your plan's monthly usage, then top-ups. A model of your own costs nothing on Aristra. See [Credits and balance](/account/credits).
