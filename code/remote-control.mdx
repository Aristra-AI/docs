---
title: "Remote Control"
description: "Continue a chat on your computer from your phone or app.aristralabs.com, or start one there. The work runs on your computer."
---

Remote Control shares a chat that runs on your computer with your Aristra account, so you can read it, send messages and answer approvals from your phone or app.aristralabs.com. The code and the commands stay on your computer; only the conversation travels.

<Note>
Remote Control is different from a [cloud chat](/code/cloud-chats), which runs on Aristra's servers. With Remote Control, your computer does the work.
</Note>

Remote Control comes with a [plan](/account/plans), because the shared chat is kept in your account. Without one, the terminal and Aristra Desktop say so when you turn it on.

## Turn it on

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Terminal">
    In a chat, run:

    ```text
    /remote-control
    ```

    Aristra Code prints the chat's link. `/rc` is the short form, and `/remote-control off` stops sharing.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Aristra Desktop">
    Right-click a chat that runs on this Mac and choose **Continue on phone and web**. A phone icon marks shared chats in the sidebar. Choose **Stop sharing to phone and web** to stop.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Remote Control needs this computer signed in with a device key. If it isn't, the menu says so: sign in again on this computer.

## What you can do from your phone and the web

- Read the chat as it runs, steps included.
- Send messages. A message sent while a reply runs waits and runs after it, as one typed on your computer would.
- Approve or decline steps. Each approval from another device needs a fresh sign-in check there, "don't ask again" answers aren't offered, and an answer given on your computer wins.
- Switch the chat's mode and model, among the ones your computer offers. A less strict mode asks for a fresh sign-in first.
- Attach files to a message.

## When your computer is away

A shared chat stays shared when the terminal closes or the runtime stops. What you send from your phone or the web meanwhile waits, marked as waiting, and runs when your computer is back. The chat's transcript stays in your account until you delete it, even after sharing stops.

## Start a chat on your computer from the web or phone

You can also start a new chat on one of your computers without being at it. On app.aristralabs.com, open **Where it runs** above a new chat, pick the computer, pick a folder, and type your message. The chat runs on that computer, with its files, shell and tools, and is saved to your account like any shared chat.

Nothing can start until you allow it **on the computer itself**. It is off by default, and the website can never turn it on.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Aristra Desktop">
    Open **Settings › General › From the web and phone** and turn on **Start chats on this Mac from the web and phone**. Then turn on each folder a chat may start in.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Terminal">
    ```text
    /remote-control computer on
    /remote-control folder on
    ```

    The first turns it on for this computer. The second allows the folder you are in. `/remote-control computer` shows what is allowed, and `off` in place of `on` undoes either.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **A folder must be trusted first** | Open a chat in the folder on the computer and trust it. An untrusted folder can't be allowed |
| **Some folders are never allowed** | Your home folder itself, a hidden folder in it, and the disk's root |
| **The website names a folder, never a path** | It sees the folder's name. The path stays on your computer |
| **The computer checks every start** | The switch, the folder, trust and the mode are checked again on the computer each time |
| **No bypass from another device** | You choose Manual, Accept edits, Plan or Auto, or leave the computer's own default |
| **The computer must be online** | A start needs the computer on and connected. Messages you send later wait for it, as for any shared chat |

Approvals wait in the chat, on the web and on the computer; the first answer wins. The computer shows a notification when a chat starts from another device.

## A computer that is always reachable

A [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) is a computer of your own in Aristra's cloud. You start chats on it from the web and your phone, and it wakes for your messages, so it is never away.

## How it connects

Your computer keeps an outbound HTTPS connection to Aristra, authenticated with its device token and device key. Nothing listens on your computer, and each request from your phone or the web runs at most once.
