A connector links one of your apps to your Aristra account, so Aristra can read and act in it: find an email, add a calendar event, update a Notion page, open a GitHub issue. Connectors come with a [plan](/account/plans).

## The apps

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Outlook, Slack, Discord, Notion, Airtable, Linear, Jira, Trello, GitHub, Sentry, Datadog, Databricks, Supabase, Figma, Canva, Dropbox, Calendly, Zoom, YouTube, HubSpot, Attio, Mailchimp, Stripe, LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and X.

## Connect an app

1. On the website, open **Customize › Connectors**.
2. Choose an app and **Connect**.
3. Sign in to the app on its own page and allow access. You come back to Connectors when it is done.

An app whose access expired shows **Needs reconnect**; choose **Reconnect**. **Disconnect** removes Aristra's access to it.

For each of an app's actions you can choose **Always allow**, **Needs approval** or **Blocked**.

## Where Aristra uses them

- **Chats** on the website, your phone, Aristra Desktop's Chat tab and [Telegram](/surfaces/telegram).
- **[Aristra Code](/surfaces/aristra-code)** on your computer, signed in. Aristra's servers make each call, so the apps' sign-ins never reach your computer.
- **[Scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks)**, which can also start when something happens in a connected app.

## MCP servers for chats

Under **Customize › Connectors**, you can also add an MCP server of your own by its address, with headers or the server's own sign-in. Until you trust it, Aristra asks before each of its tools runs; once you trust it, its read-only tools run without asking. Your chats on the website and Telegram use it.

MCP servers and skills for Aristra Code are set up on your computer. See [MCP servers](/code/extend/mcp) and [Skills](/code/extend/skills).

## Use Aristra from other AI apps

**Customize › MCP** gives you an address to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant that takes a custom connector, so it can use your Aristra memory and files. Its use is paid from your balance.
