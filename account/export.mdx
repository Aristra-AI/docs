## Devices

**Devices** lists every computer signed in to your account: Aristra Desktop, the Aristra CLI and a cloud computer. Each shows whether it is online and when it was last seen.

**Sign out** removes a computer from your account. Its chats and files stay on it; it has to sign in again to use your account.

## Downloads

**Downloads** has Aristra Desktop for Mac and the command that installs the Aristra CLI:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://aristralabs.com/install.sh | sh
```

Both are free and work with a model of your own. See [Set up Aristra Code](/code/setup).

## Export

**Import and export** gives you your account's data as one zip file. It needs no plan, so you can always take what you made, also after a plan ends.

| In the file | |
|---|---|
| `chats/` | Every chat saved to your account, each as a Markdown file, with a list of them all |
| `memory/` | What Aristra remembers about you and your work, as a readable file and as data |
| `files/files.csv` | The list of your files: name, type, size and date, and the chat a file was attached to |
| `usage.csv` | A year of usage rows |
| `README.txt` | What is in the file, and what is not |

Kept somewhere else:

- **Your files themselves** download from **Files**.
- **Craft documents** export from each document's menu, as Markdown, Word or PDF.
- **Chats on a computer** that you did not share live on that computer, in `~/.aristra`. They never reached your account.
- **A cloud computer's** chats and memory export from its own page. See [Cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer).
