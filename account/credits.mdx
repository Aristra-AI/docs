Everything Aristra charges for comes out of one balance, in US dollars. It has up to three parts:

| Part | Where it comes from | Spent | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly usage** | Your plan: $20 on Plus, $100 on Pro, $200 on Max | First | Until your billing date. What is left does not carry over |
| **Top-up balance** | What you add, any amount from $10 to $500 | After the month's usage | 12 months from each top-up |
| **Starter credit** | $1, once, for a new account | First, on models and tools | Until it is used |

You can top up with or without a plan. A top-up adds exactly what you pay: $25 adds $25, with no bonus and no fee.

## Top up

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Aristra One">
    Open **Billing** (`app.aristralabs.com/account/billing`). Under **Top up**, type an amount or use the buttons, which add to the amount, then choose **Top up**. Payment is on Stripe's page.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Aristra Desktop">
    Open **Settings › Billing**. Under **Top up**, choose an amount or type another one. Stripe's page opens in your browser, and your balance updates when you come back to the app.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The money arrives in your balance when the payment does, usually within a few seconds.

## What your balance pays for

- Aristra's models and hosted tools, like your plan's monthly usage, once that is used.
- Without a plan, chats on Aristra One on the web and your phone, and Aristra's models and tools on your computer, for as long as your starter credit and top-up balance last. A [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) of your own runs on top-ups (making one needs $2 in your balance). Memory in your account, Craft and connectors need a [plan](/account/plans).

Under $5 left, your account pages, the Aristra CLI's `/usage` and Aristra Desktop say **Low balance**, so a long run does not stop short.

## A new account's limit

A new account can add up to **$100** in total at first. The limit lifts once a payment of $5 or more has settled, which takes a few days for a card. After that each top-up can be up to $500. A plan's own payment counts toward lifting the limit and never against the $100.

Billing shows what you can add right now.

## The starter credit

Every new account gets a **$1 starter credit**, once for each email address. It pays for Aristra's models and hosted tools on the web, your phone and your computer, so you can try chats on Aristra One before you top up. It is spent before the rest of your balance, never on a cloud computer, and it is part of the balance your account pages show.

## Refunds

Ask [support](mailto:support@aristralabs.com). A refunded top-up takes back what is left of it.
