One Aristra account works everywhere. Aristra on your own computer is free. A plan adds what Aristra hosts for you.

## The parts of Aristra

| Name | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Aristra One** | Your account's plan: Free, Plus, Pro or Max. Its pages on the website show your plan, balance, usage and devices |
| **The website** | [app.aristralabs.com](https://app.aristralabs.com): your chats in any browser or on a phone (paid from your balance, or with a plan), your files, your memory and Craft. See [The web and your phone](/surfaces/web) |
| **Aristra Desktop** | The app for Mac: chats, and the Code tab |
| **Aristra CLI** | The `aristra` command in a terminal, on a Mac or a Linux machine |
| **Aristra Code** | The coding agent. You use it in Aristra Desktop's Code tab, in the Aristra CLI, in your editor, and on a cloud computer |
| **Aristra Cloud** | Everything Aristra hosts: Aristra's models, the cloud agent, a cloud computer, account memory (every account's), storage, connectors and scheduled cloud tasks |

## What is free

- **Aristra Desktop and the Aristra CLI**, signed in to your account, with a model of your own: your own provider key, your ChatGPT plan, or a model running on your computer. See [Models](/code/configure/models).
- **Aristra Code** in your own folders, with every tool that runs on your computer.
- **Your account's memory**, on every device: what Aristra remembers about you, saved as you chat and learned each night from your recent chats. Saving, importing and searching it cost nothing. See [Memory](/memory).
- **Files** in Aristra Desktop, on the web, and `/files` in the terminal: what your chats made, shared or were given. Reading PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files on your computer. In the cloud, a scanned page costs $0.006 to read ($0.015 with vision).
- **Craft**, on the website and in Aristra Desktop: docs you write with Aristra or yourself, kept in your account. See [Craft](/craft).
- **Web search in chats on the web and your phone**. From your computer a search is $0.0075 from your balance, and with your balance used up, free public search (Exa, Parallel, Firecrawl or Keenable) answers unless you turn it off.
- **A $1 starter credit**, once, when you create your account. It pays for Aristra's models and hosted tools, so you can chat on the web and your phone before you top up.
- Your account pages on the website: Overview, Profile, Personalization, Billing, Usage, Devices, Downloads, Import and export, Permissions, Agent payments, Cloud computer and Apps.

You can also [top up your balance](/account/credits) without a plan. It pays for chats on the web and your phone, Aristra's models and hosted tools on your computer, and a cloud computer.

## What a plan adds

| | Free account | With a plan |
|---|---|---|
| On the web and phone | Chats, projects, files and Craft, with chats paid from your balance | Everything: chats, projects, files, Craft |
| Aristra's models | Every model, from your balance | Every model, from your monthly usage |
| Remote Control and shared chats | No | Yes |
| Chats in the cloud | Yes, paid from your balance | Yes |
| Connectors, scheduled cloud tasks, channels | No | Yes |
| Cloud computer | Up to Large, paid from top-ups | Yes, up to your plan's size |
| Files storage | 1 GB | From 10 GB |
| Craft documents in your account | Yes | Yes |

The plans, their prices and their limits are on [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).

## Your account pages

Every setting of your account is on these pages. Open **Settings** from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or go to `app.aristralabs.com/account`. A project's own settings (its instructions, and with a plan its API keys) are on the project's page.

| Page | What is there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | What your account has, and the way to each part of it |
| **Profile** | Your name, email and account ID, and deleting your account |
| **Personalization** | Theme and font, how readily Aristra acts on its own, memory, your timezone and location |
| **Billing** | Your balance in dollars, topping up, the plans and their comparison, your card and invoices on Stripe |
| **Usage** | Where your money went, by model and by tool, the tool prices, and the search Aristra uses |
| **Devices** | Every computer signed in to your account, and signing one out |
| **Downloads** | Aristra Desktop and the Aristra CLI |
| **Import and export** | Your account's data as one file, and the sources Aristra will import from |
| **Permissions** | What Aristra in the cloud can do before asking you, and the sites its cloud browser stays signed in to |
| **Agent payments** | The wallet Aristra can pay with when you ask it to buy something |
| **Connectors** | With a plan: the apps linked to your account. It opens **Customize › Connectors** |
| **Cloud computer** | Your own machine in Aristra Cloud, with a plan or on top-ups: making it, chatting with it, and its settings |
| **Apps** | The [sites](/sites) Aristra published for you, the previews it started in your chats and the apps it runs on your cloud computer, each at an address only you can open, unless you share it with people by email |
| **Scheduled tasks** | With a plan: the tasks Aristra runs in the cloud on a schedule (**Scheduled tasks**, in the website's sidebar). On your own computer they are free |
| **Storage** | With a plan: how much of your account's room your files take |
| **Workspace** | Your workspace's name and icon, its people and its audit log. Inviting others needs a team workspace, which isn't available yet |

Everyone can read [Models and prices](https://app.aristralabs.com/models), with or without an account.
