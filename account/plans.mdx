Every plan includes its price in usage each month: Plus is $20 a month and includes $20 of usage. Every model is on every plan, at its provider's own price. A Free account chats on the web and phone too, paid from its balance.

## The plans

| | Free | Plus | Pro | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price a month | $0 | $20 | $100 | $200 |
| Aristra Desktop, the CLI and Aristra Code on your computer | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| On the web and phone | Chats, from your balance | Full | Full | Full |
| Usage included each month | None | $20 | $100 | $200 |
| Aristra's models | All, from your balance | All | All | All |
| Web search and other hosted tools | From your balance | From your usage | From your usage | From your usage |
| Memory in your account, on every device | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Chat sync and remote access to your computers | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Connectors, scheduled tasks in the cloud and channels | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Cloud computer | From top-ups, up to 4 GB, 100 GB disk | Up to 4 GB, 25 GB disk | Up to 4 GB, 100 GB disk | Up to 8 GB, 100 GB disk |
| [Public sites and apps](/sites#make-it-public), at a name of your own | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Files storage | 1 GB | 10 GB | 100 GB | 200 GB |
| Craft documents in your account | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Chats at once | 4 | 6 | 10 | 16 |
| Top-ups, any amount from $10 | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A new account also gets a $1 [starter credit](/account/credits#the-starter-credit), once. It pays for models and tools, so you can try chats on the web before you top up.

Choose or change a plan on **Billing**, at `app.aristralabs.com/account/billing`. Payment goes through Stripe.

## What your usage pays for

Your plan's monthly usage, and your [top-up balance](/account/credits) after it, pay for:

- **Aristra's models**, by the tokens a call uses, at the provider's own price. Each model's price is on [Models and prices](https://app.aristralabs.com/models).
- **Hosted tools**: Brave search, web page reads, the code sandbox and cloud browser, images, transcription and document reading. See [Usage and prices](/account/usage).
- **A cloud computer**: the time it is awake, and its disk whether it is awake or asleep. See [Cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer).

A model of your own (your key, your ChatGPT plan, a model on your computer) costs nothing on Aristra.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does my usage reset?">
    On your billing date each month. Usage left over does not carry over. Your top-up balance is separate: each top-up lasts 12 months and is used after the month's usage runs out.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens at zero?">
    Aristra stops before the next paid step and says so. A call that is already running finishes and is charged in full, so your balance can dip a few cents below zero; your next charge, top-up or month pays that back first. [Top up](/account/credits) from $10, switch the chat to a model of your own with `/model`, or wait for the reset. With a plan, a cloud computer goes to sleep and keeps its disk. Without one, when your top-up balance runs out it is stopped and its disk kept for 30 days; a top-up within them brings it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I change plan?">
    An upgrade starts at once, and you get the difference between the two plans' monthly usage, in proportion to the time left in the billing period. A downgrade starts at the end of the billing period. A cloud computer larger than the new plan allows moves down to its largest size then; its disk stays as it is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Billing say every plan is taken?">
    Plans open in batches while Aristra One is new. When a batch is taken, Billing says so before you pay, and nothing is charged. Your account keeps working as it is, and more plans open soon.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a limit on how fast I can use my monthly usage?">
    No. It is one amount with no daily or weekly limit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When you subscribe

Your computers follow the account's plan within a few minutes. The day a plan starts:

- **Aristra Desktop offers, once,** to share chats on this Mac to your phone and the web, and to let chats in the cloud read a folder here. Each is optional. Later, the same choices are in a chat's menu (**Continue on phone and web**) and a folder's menu (**Use from other devices**).
- **In the terminal**, `/remote-control` shares the chat you are in.

## After a plan ends

Your account stays, as a Free account, and nothing you made is deleted.

| | What happens |
|---|---|
| Chats | All still here. New chats on the web are paid from your balance |
| Craft | All still here, and you keep writing, as every account does |
| Files | All still here, to open and download. With more than a Free account's 1 GB, new files are refused until you remove some; nothing is removed for you |
| Account memory | Still yours on every device, and Aristra keeps learning, as it does for every account |
| Cloud computer | Keeps running from your top-up balance, at up to Large (4 GB); a larger one moves down to Large. With none left, stopped: its disk is kept for 30 days, and you get an email a week before it is deleted |
| Public sites and apps | Private within a few minutes: only you can open them. Their people and their names are kept; after you renew, make them public again in **Apps** |
| Scheduled cloud tasks and channels | Paused. They start again when you renew |
| Remote Control and your computers | Shared chats stay readable. Following, answering and starting chats on your computers from the web stop until you renew |
| Connectors | Still listed. You can't connect or change them until you renew |
| Project API keys | Stop working until you renew |
| Top-up balance | Still yours. It pays for chats on the web and for Aristra's models and tools on your computer |
| Aristra Desktop and the Aristra CLI | Keep working with your own model |

Renew on **Billing** to get everything back as it was.
