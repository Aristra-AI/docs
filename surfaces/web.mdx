[app.aristralabs.com](https://app.aristralabs.com) is Aristra in any browser, on a computer or a phone. Chats there run on Aristra's cloud agent and are kept in your account, so they are the same chats in Aristra Desktop's Chat tab and in the terminal's [cloud chats](/code/cloud-chats).

## What is in the sidebar

| | What it is for | |
|---|---|---|
| **Chats** | [Chats](/chats) with any of Aristra's models, paid from your balance | Every account |
| **Projects** | [Projects](/projects): a piece of work's chats, files, instructions and memory | Every account |
| **Files** | [Files](/files) your account keeps | Every account |
| **Memory** | What Aristra remembers about you. See [Memory](/memory) | Every account |
| **Craft** | [Craft](/craft) docs | With a plan |
| **Scheduled tasks** | [Scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks) in the cloud | With a plan |
| **Customize** | [Connectors](/connectors), Skills, Channels ([Telegram](/surfaces/telegram)) and MCP | With a plan |
| **Cloud computer** | Your [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) | With a plan or a top-up balance |

Without a plan, the items that need one aren't in the sidebar, and their pages say what a plan adds if you open one from a link. After a plan ends, they stay, to read and export. When your balance runs out, the message box says so and offers a top-up or a plan.

## Your account

**Settings**, from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, opens your account's pages: your profile, personalization and memory switches, billing, usage, devices, downloads and export. See [Your account and Aristra One](/account/overview).

## Your computers

Under a new chat, **Runs on Aristra Cloud** lists your cloud computer and every computer signed in to your account. With a plan, choose one to:

- **Follow and answer a chat** you shared from Aristra Desktop or the terminal, including its approvals. See [Remote Control](/code/remote-control).
- **Start a chat on a computer**, in a folder that computer allows. It is off until you turn it on, on that computer. See [Start a chat on your computer from the web or phone](/code/remote-control#start-a-chat-on-your-computer-from-the-web-or-phone).

The chat runs on that computer, with its files and tools, on one of Aristra's models, paid from your plan's monthly usage and then your top-up balance.

## On a phone

Open app.aristralabs.com in your phone's browser and sign in. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app. Everything above works there, including answering approvals for chats on your computer.

## Workspaces

Your account has a personal workspace. Inviting other people needs a team workspace, which isn't available yet.
