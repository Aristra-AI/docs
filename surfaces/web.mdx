[app.aristralabs.com](https://app.aristralabs.com) is Aristra in any browser, on a computer or a phone. Chats there run on Aristra's cloud agent and are kept in your account, so they are the same chats in Aristra Desktop and in the terminal's [cloud chats](/code/cloud-chats).

Without a plan, chats on the website are paid from your [balance](/account/credits) as you go: a new account's $1 starter credit first, then top-ups. Every model and Aristra's hosted tools are there, at the prices on [Usage and prices](/account/usage). A [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) of your own runs on top-ups. You can add files to a chat or to **Files** in the sidebar, which lists everything you added and everything Aristra made for you; a Free account has 1 GB of room for them. Your memory is kept in your account too. What a plan adds there: Craft, more room for files, connectors, scheduled tasks, channels and your other computers. Those pages say so until you have one. When your balance runs out, the chat says so and offers a top-up or a plan. See [What is free](/account/overview#what-is-free).

## What is in the sidebar

| | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Chats** | Talk to Aristra with any model, paid from your balance. The cloud agent can browse, run code in its own sandbox, make images, read documents and use your connected apps |
| **Projects** | A piece of work with its own instructions and memory, shared by every chat in it. A project can have a main folder on each of your computers |
| **Craft** | With a plan, documents you write with Aristra, with comments, version history and an outline |
| **Files** | The files kept in your account: what you added, what you attached to a chat and what Aristra made, with the chat each came from. Search them by name or by the words in them, show one project's files, rename, star, and send files to the trash and back. With a plan, **Published pages** lists the pages you shared |
| **Scheduled tasks** | With a plan, work Aristra runs for you in the cloud on a schedule or when something happens. See [Scheduled tasks](#scheduled-tasks) |
| **Memory** | What Aristra remembers about you, on every device, with or without a plan, including what it learns from your chats each night. You can read and delete it. While the Memory switch is off, nothing is used or saved; once it is back on, Aristra may learn from chats still in your history. An incognito chat is never learned from |
| **Customize** | Connectors, Skills, Channels and MCP servers for the cloud agent |
| **Cloud computer** | Your own machine in Aristra's cloud. See [Cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer) |

Your files, and the files in your chats, count against your account's storage: 1 GB on a Free account, 10 GB on Plus, 100 GB on Pro, 200 GB on Max. When it is full, a new file is refused and says why; nothing you have is removed. **Storage** in your account shows how much is used.

## Connect your apps

**Connectors** link apps such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, GitHub, Notion and Linear to your account. Cloud chats and Aristra Code can then read and act in them. Open **Customize › Connectors**, choose an app and sign in to it there. Disconnecting removes Aristra's access.

**Customize** also has **MCP servers** and **Skills** for the cloud agent. MCP servers and skills for Aristra Code on your computer are set up on that computer; see [MCP servers](/code/extend/mcp) and [Skills](/code/extend/skills).

## Telegram

Under **Customize › Channels**, connect Telegram and message Aristra from a private Telegram chat. Each Telegram chat is a chat in your account. With a [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer), you can send Telegram messages to it instead. WhatsApp, Slack and Discord are listed as coming soon.

## Scheduled tasks

A scheduled task in the cloud is a prompt Aristra runs there, on a schedule or when something happens, with your computer off. It comes with a [plan](/account/plans). Each run is a chat you can open. A run that needs your answer waits in **Scheduled tasks** until you give it. Runs are paid from your balance like any other chat, and they pause when a plan ends.

A run with nothing new to report says so quietly: it is kept in the task's runs and nothing is sent to you. A run that could not start (no balance left, or the plan ended) or that failed always tells you, where the task's results go. After three failures in a row a task is stopped, and the third notice says so; turn it back on under **Scheduled tasks** once the cause is fixed.

The page was called Automations; its address is the same.

Work on your own computer is scheduled there instead, in Aristra Desktop or by asking Aristra Code in plain words. See [Scheduled tasks](/code/guides/background#scheduled-tasks).

## Share

A Craft document, or a page Aristra made, can be published from its **Share** menu to a link anyone can read. Everything you published is under **Published pages** in Files. **Unpublish** in the same menu stops the link working for everyone.

## Your computers

The website lists every computer signed in to your account. From it you can:

- **Follow and answer a chat** you shared from Aristra Desktop or the terminal, including its approvals. See [Remote Control](/code/remote-control).
- **Start a chat on a computer**, in a folder that computer allows. It is off until you turn it on, on that computer. See [Start a chat on your computer from the web or phone](/code/remote-control#start-a-chat-on-your-computer-from-the-web-or-phone).

The chat runs on that computer, with its files and tools, and its model calls are paid from your plan's monthly usage.

## On a phone

Open app.aristralabs.com in your phone's browser and sign in. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app. Everything above works there, including answering approvals for chats on your computer.

## Teams

A workspace can have other people in it. Invite them under **Settings › Workspace › People**, and choose what each can do. Each person signs in with their own Aristra account, and each person's own plan decides what they can use.
