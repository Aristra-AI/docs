Aristra Desktop is the Aristra app for Mac (Apple silicon and Intel). Download it from your account's **Downloads** page and sign in, in your browser or with a code. Its sidebar has two tabs:

- **Chat**: your [chats](/chats) without a folder, with Files and Craft. These chats run in Aristra Cloud (a chat beside a doc kept on this Mac runs on this Mac) and are kept in your account, so they are the same chats as on the website and your phone. They are paid from your [balance](/account/credits): a new account's starter credit, your plan's monthly usage, then top-ups.
- **Code**: [Aristra Code](/code/desktop). Your folders with their chats, and everything you use while coding beside the chat.

Switch between them with the switch at the top of the sidebar, or with **⌥⌘←** and **⌥⌘→**. The tab you are not on shows a dot when a chat there waits for you (amber) or is working (green), or when the Code tab has scheduled runs you haven't read (accent color). Opening any chat, from search, a notification or the Dock, moves to its tab.

Aristra Desktop includes Aristra Code, so you don't need to install the Aristra CLI separately. Both use the same runtime and the same `~/.aristra` folder: a chat started in the terminal shows in the Code tab, and the other way round.

## Start a chat

A new chat in the Chat tab runs in the cloud, so you need to be signed in. On a new chat:

- **Project**: a [project](/projects), whose instructions and memory the chat uses.
- **Incognito** (the ghost at the top right of a new chat): a chat that isn't in your history or memory, and nothing of it is kept on this Mac. See [Incognito chats](/chats#incognito-chats).
- The **microphone** button: dictate up to 10 minutes, typed for you and paid from your balance.

**Quick entry** (**⌥Space**, from anywhere, or **Aristra › Quick Entry**) opens a small box: type and press Enter to start a chat without opening the window.

## Files and Craft

**Files** lists the files your chats on this Mac shared, made or changed, and, signed in, the files in your account. See [Files](/files#in-aristra-desktop).

**Craft** holds your docs, written with Aristra or yourself, kept in your account. A doc you start while Aristra Desktop can't reach your account is kept on this Mac until you move it. See [Craft](/craft).

## Find something you said

Press **⌘K** and type. Under the chats and commands whose names match, **In messages** lists what was said in any chat on this Mac that holds your words. Choose one to open its chat. The search runs on this Mac and covers this Mac's chats. On the website, the Chats page finds cloud chats by their title.

## Your plan and account

**Settings › Aristra One**, **Aristra One** in the account menu (your name, bottom left) or in **⌘K** opens your account's pages on app.aristralabs.com in a new tab of your browser: the plans, your balance, spending by day, your devices, the cloud computer and export. **Settings › Billing** shows your plan, the plans and top-ups, and **Settings › Usage** your balance and spending, in dollars; without a running plan, **See plans** opens the plans on the website. Payments always happen on Stripe's page in your browser. See [Plans](/account/plans).

## Memory

**Settings › Memory** has your account's Memory switches, **See and edit your memory** (on the website), **Import from Codex and Claude Code**, and a **Memory folder** that keeps a copy of your memory as files in `~/Aristra/memory`. See [Memory](/memory).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| ⌘N | New chat |
| ⌥⌘← / ⌥⌘→ | Chat / Code |
| ⌘K | Search chats, their messages, and commands |
| ⌘B | Show or hide the sidebar |
| ⌘J | Terminal |
| ⌥⌘B | Review |
| ⇧⌘B | Browser |
| ⌘, | Settings |
| ⌥Space | Quick entry, from anywhere (configurable) |
| ⌘W | Close a chat window |
| Return (or ⌘Return) | Send, as set in Settings › General |
| ⇧Return | New line |
| ↑ | Recall your last prompt, in an empty box |
| Esc | Stop Aristra while it works |
| ⌘-click | Open a link the other way |

In the Code tab, more shortcuts open the panels beside a chat. See [Aristra Desktop's Code tab](/code/desktop).
