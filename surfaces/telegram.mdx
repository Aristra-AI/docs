With a [plan](/account/plans), you can message Aristra from Telegram. It is the same Aristra, with your memory, your files and your connected apps, and its replies are paid from your balance like any chat.

## Connect Telegram

1. On the website, open **Customize › Channels** and choose **Connect** on Telegram.
2. Open the link, or scan the QR code with your phone, and press **Start** in Telegram. The link works once, for 5 minutes.

**Disconnect**, on the same card, stops Aristra answering there.

## Your Telegram chat

Your private Telegram chat with Aristra continues one chat in your account, named **Aristra**. It isn't in the website's sidebar; **Chats** lists it. Send `/new` to start a separate chat, which shows in the sidebar marked as from Telegram, and `/home` to go back. A Telegram group gets a chat of its own.

Aristra reads voice notes you send.

| Command | |
|---|---|
| `/new` | Start a new chat |
| `/home` | Go back to your main chat |
| `/model` | Choose the model |
| `/stop` | Stop the reply that's running |
| `/help` | The commands |

## Send it to your cloud computer

With a [cloud computer](/surfaces/cloud-computer), turn on **Telegram messages go to this computer** on its page. Your private Telegram chat then runs on it, waking it when it sleeps. Send `/new` for a fresh chat there. Approvals are answered on the website.

## Other apps

WhatsApp, Slack and Discord are listed under **Channels** as coming soon. Today only Telegram is live.
